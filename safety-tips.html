💡 Safety Tips

Practical, empowering safety guidance — not fear-based, but awareness-focused.

Safety tip categories

🧍‍♀️

Personal Safety

  • ✅

    Trust your instincts — if a situation feels uncomfortable, it is okay to leave.

  • ✅

    Keep a fully charged phone with you whenever possible. Consider carrying a power bank.

  • ✅

    Share your plans with at least one trusted person — where you are going and when you expect to return.

  • ✅

    Memorise at least one emergency number: 112 (National), 100 (Police), 1091 (Women's Helpline).

  • ✅

    Avoid displaying expensive jewellery or devices in unfamiliar areas.

✈️

Travel Safety

  • ✅

    Research your destination before travelling — know the local emergency numbers and safe areas.

  • ✅

    Use official registered taxis or verified ride-sharing apps. Note the driver details before starting.

  • ✅

    Share your live location with a trusted contact whenever travelling alone at night.

  • ✅

    Keep digital and physical copies of important documents when travelling.

  • ✅

    Identify safe public places along your route — hotels, hospitals, police stations.

🚌

Public Transport

  • ✅

    Prefer well-lit, busy stops and boarding points. Avoid isolated boarding areas at night.

  • ✅

    Sit near the driver or in designated women's sections where available.

  • ✅

    Keep your belongings secure and close to your body on crowded transport.

  • ✅

    Be aware of emergency exits and alarm buttons in metro/bus systems.

🌐

Online Safety

  • ✅

    Review privacy settings on all social media regularly. Limit who can see your location and personal details.

  • ✅

    Do not share real-time location on public social posts — post after you have left the location.

  • ✅

    Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication on important accounts.

  • ✅

    Be cautious of people you meet only online. Never share financial information with strangers.

  • ✅

    Report online harassment or threatening content to the platform and at cybercrime.gov.in.

🆘

Emergency Preparedness

  • ✅

    Save emergency contacts in your phone under an easily findable name (e.g., "ICE - Emergency").

  • ✅

    Discuss a safety plan with trusted friends or family — let them know your check-in routine.

  • ✅

    Learn basic self-defence awareness — many community centres offer free or subsidised classes.

  • ✅

    Keep a small emergency kit handy: phone charger, emergency cash, personal safety device.

🔐

Cyber Safety

  • ✅

    Beware of phishing links sent via messages — verify before clicking, even if from known contacts.

  • ✅

    Keep your device's operating system and apps updated to reduce security vulnerabilities.

  • ✅

    Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive transactions. Use a VPN on untrusted networks.

  • ✅

    Cyber stalking and online harassment are criminal offences. Do not hesitate to report.
