Safety Awareness

Stay Informed. Stay Prepared.

👁️

Stay Situationally Aware

Being aware of your surroundings is the single most effective safety measure. Trust your instincts and plan your routes in advance, especially at night.

📲

Keep Contacts Updated

Regularly inform trusted contacts of your location and plans, especially during late hours or travel to unfamiliar places.

🔢

Know Local Emergency Numbers

Memorise essential numbers: 112 (National Emergency), 100 (Police), 1091 (Women's Helpline). Save them in your phone and SafeHer contacts.

🗺️

Use Location Sharing Responsibly

Share your live location only with people you completely trust. Review who has access and revoke it when it is no longer needed.
